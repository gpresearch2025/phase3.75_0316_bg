Technical Snapshot

The shortest accurate way to describe the product

Developer review ready
What it is Consentext is the policy and routing layer in front of health-context use.

It decides what stays internal, what may leave in reduced form, what needs explicit authorization, and what must fail closed.

Why it matters It makes disclosure behavior explicit, testable, and auditable.

The platform turns vague trust promises into route selection, transform rules, recipient checks, and append-only proof.

Non-negotiable contract Private is internal-only, Private+ is narrow, and broader reasoning is a separate governed lane.

That separation is the core system contract, and the rest of the prototype is organized around enforcing it consistently.

Control plane first

The governance layer sits

between the vault and every AI interaction.

Vault stores the user's health context while the trust kernel decides what may participate, what must be minimized, and what is blocked.

Data plane Vault, health context, experience

Health portals, wearable syncing, and lab visualizations evolve while the control plane is defined.

Control plane Consent, policy, gateway, audit

Phase 3.75 centers on a trust kernel that governs consent, lane routing, clinician sharing, and auditability before exports leave Consentext.

Phase 3.75

We treat the vault as conceptual context and the control plane as the specific architecture target. The AI gateway mediates every prompt, keeps the Private lane on an internal protected model, and handles governed external routing without collapsing Private+ and Max Intelligence into the same promise.

                Developer Handoff
                
          
                The first surfaces to build without overcommitting
                
        
                
        
                
          
                1. Internal protected model surface
                Keep the Private lane visibly mandatory in Phase 3.75 and route sensitive context through it without external export.
                
          
                2. Consent and share lifecycle
                Create, preview, revoke, and audit every outbound share object across AI, clinician, and API recipients.
                
          
                3. User-authorized clinician sharing
                Support doctor, clinic, and hospital sharing architecturally now while leaving the full staff portal deferred.
                
          
                4. Adapter-ready AI gateway routing
                Classify prompt, preserve distinct Private+ and Max Intelligence behaviors, and keep provider count decisions open until pricing lands.
                
          
                5. Minimal internal ops console
                Provide audit lookup, troubleshooting, and operational visibility without implying a full internal admin product.
                
          
                6. Audit viewer and trust proof
                Keep the trust trail legible to users and operators while the open decisions remain marked as open.
