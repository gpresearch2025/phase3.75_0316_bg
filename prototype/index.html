March 17 Source Of Truth

Settled direction versus open decisions

This prototype now treats March 17 as the leading memo. Settled items shape the experience directly, and unresolved items stay visibly open so the build does not hard-code them by accident.

Working direction

Use these assumptions now

Source of truth
  • Locked
    Private lane requires an internal protected model.

    Phase 3.75 keeps a protected model inside Consentext-controlled systems.

  • Working
    Private+ and Max Intelligence stay separate.

    They share the gateway framework but make different promises about disclosure and capability.

  • Locked
    Patient-authorized clinician sharing matters now.

    Doctor, clinic, and hospital sharing should be supported architecturally even while the full staff portal waits.

  • Locked
    Gateway pricing should be adapter-ready by provider count.

    Ask for the base plus 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 provider implementations instead of guessing a single-provider shape.

  • Working
    Integrations are architecture-now, implementation-later.

    Interfaces and adapter patterns should exist now even if wearable, vendor, and EHR rollouts happen in later waves.

Open decision

Keep these visibly unresolved

Do not hard-code
  • Open
    Minimal internal ops console scope

    Audit lookup, troubleshooting, and operational visibility are likely needed, but broader account or config tooling is still undecided.

  • Open
    UI ownership pending Joyel update

    The prototype should not imply Jordan is locked into owning the full front-end or UI delivery surface.

  • Open
    Reference integration pattern now versus architecture only

    One reference implementation may help later, but the immediate requirement is an integration-aware architecture.

  • Open
    Max Intelligence final scope if pricing bites

    Target it for Phase 3.75, but leave room for a conscious tradeoff if pricing forces it.

      Scope Clarification

      What this phase is and is not

      Phase 3.75 is

      • Trust boundary definition
      • Internal protected model for the Private lane
      • Consent engine and policy evaluation
      • De-identification and privacy safeguards
      • Patient-authorized clinician sharing architecture
      • Adapter-ready AI gateway architecture
      • Minimal internal ops and audit visibility
      • Outbound governance and audit traceability

      Phase 3.75 is not

      • A full clinician-facing staff portal in the first pass
      • A full wearable or EHR implementation pass right now
      • A forced single-provider strategy before pricing
      • A lock on UI ownership before Joyel's update
      • Judgment that every prior component must be replaced

                1. Internal protected model surface
                Keep the Private lane visibly mandatory in Phase 3.75 and route sensitive context through it without external export.
                
          
                2. Consent and share lifecycle
                Create, preview, revoke, and audit every outbound share object across AI, clinician, and API recipients.
                
          
                3. Patient-authorized clinician sharing
                Support doctor, clinic, and hospital sharing architecturally now while leaving the full staff portal deferred.
                
          
                4. Adapter-ready AI gateway routing
                Classify prompt, preserve distinct Private+ and Max Intelligence behaviors, and keep provider count decisions open until pricing lands.
                
          
                5. Minimal internal ops console
                Provide audit lookup, troubleshooting, and operational visibility without implying a full internal admin product.
                
          
                6. Audit viewer and trust proof
                Keep the trust trail legible to users and operators while the open decisions remain marked as open.
