Consentext / Phase 3.75

Executive Brief

Consentext is the governance layer between health context and AI. It helps people use AI, share safely with clinicians, and keep a visible proof trail without treating every request the same way.

What it is

A trust and routing layer in front of health-context use.

Vault keeps the health context. The trust kernel decides what may stay internal, what may leave in reduced form, and what must be blocked or explicitly authorized.

Who it is for

People who want useful AI and safe sharing without losing control.

The prototype is framed for patients first, with support for patient-authorized clinician sharing and a minimal internal ops console for support and audit lookup.

Key distinction

Private and Private+ are not the same promise.

Private means the explanation stays inside Consentext on a protected internal model. Private+ means outside help gets only the minimum needed through a governed route.

Why it matters

Trust becomes a product feature, not a legal afterthought.

The value is not only the answer from AI. The value is the visible control boundary, the lane decision, the patient share controls, and the proof of what happened.

Feature buckets

  • Core concept: vault context separated from governance and outbound routing.
  • Primary workflow: policy creation, lane selection, governed gateway, and audit trail.
  • Supporting controls: clinician share path, recipient verification, and ops lookup.
  • Proof: downloadable policy, audit summary, clinician packet preview, and narrated walkthrough.

Current locked direction

  • Private requires an internal protected model.
  • Private+ stays narrow by default.
  • Gateway remains adapter-ready while provider count and pricing stay open.
  • Patient-authorized clinician sharing is in scope before a full staff portal.

Repeat-back takeaway

Consentext makes health context useful, shareable, and auditable without forcing every request into the same level of disclosure.

It creates a safer internal path, a narrow governed outside path, a broader governed path when the user truly wants it, and a visible proof trail for every decision.
